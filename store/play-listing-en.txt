Ponos Hercegovine 2026 — schedule, live scores and alerts from the tournament

=== FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000 characters) ===
The official app of the international veterans handball tournament "Ponos Hercegovine", played in Mostar from 5 to 8 November 2026.

The tournament brings together women's and men's veteran teams from several countries. The app keeps you on top of who is playing, where, and how it ended — without digging through group chats and printed sheets.

WHAT THE APP OFFERS

Daily schedule
Time, opponent and venue for every match. If the timetable shifts, you see the new time straight away.

Live scores
The scoreboard updates while the match is being played, with nothing to refresh.

Match timeline
Goals, saves and suspensions, minute by minute.

Standings and finals
Group tables, semi-finals and the final — and, where the competition is played that way, a final group in which everyone plays everyone. The ranking rule is shown under the tables.

Statistics
Top scorers and best goalkeepers of the tournament.

Teams and squads
Players, shirt numbers and captains for every team.

Notifications you choose yourself
Follow the teams you care about and switch on only what matters to you: upcoming match, goal by your team, final whistle, schedule change, social programme and dinners.

Venue and accommodation
Addresses of the sports hall, hotels and evening events, with navigation one tap away.

Gallery
Photos from the tournament.

Croatian and English
The language can be changed inside the app at any time.

NO SIGN-UP NEEDED

Spectators do not need an account. Open the app and everything is there. Accounts exist only for team representatives, who register their players, and for the organisers, who run the matches.

ABOUT THE ORGANISER

The tournament is organised by VHMRK Zrinjski Mostar, a club that brings together former players and friends of handball, continues the handball tradition of Zrinjski and connects generations through veterans sport.
